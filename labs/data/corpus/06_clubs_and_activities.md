# Clubs and After-School Activities

## Robotics Club
Meets Tuesdays and Thursdays, 3:30–5:00 pm, in Room 214. Competes in the regional robotics league in February. Advisor: Ms. Okafor.

## Coding Club
Meets Mondays, Wednesdays, and Fridays, 3:30–4:15 pm, in the Library Lab. Beginners welcome; laptops provided.

## Debate Club
Meets Wednesdays, 3:30–4:30 pm, in Room 108. Tournaments are held on four Saturdays per term.

## AI Ethics Circle
A student-led discussion group that meets on the first Thursday of each month, 3:30–4:30 pm, in Room 120. Members review new AI tools and advise the Student Advisory Council.

## Joining
Students sign up through the student portal during the first three weeks of each term. Late sign-ups are at the advisor's discretion.

## Transport
The after-school activity bus leaves at 5:15 pm from the main entrance on Tuesdays and Thursdays only.
