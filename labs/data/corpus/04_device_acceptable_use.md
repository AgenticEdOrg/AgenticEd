# Device and Network Acceptable Use

## 1. District devices
Every student in grades 6–12 receives a district laptop. Devices remain district property and may be inspected by Technology Services.

## 2. Network filtering
The district network filters content as required by law. Attempting to bypass filters, including with VPNs or proxy services, is prohibited.

## 3. Accounts and passwords
Students must not share passwords. Passwords must be at least 12 characters. Accounts are locked after 10 failed sign-in attempts; students can unlock them at the school library help desk.

## 4. Repairs
Damaged devices should be reported to the library help desk within two school days. The first accidental-damage repair each school year is free; later repairs cost $25 each, which may be waived for families with financial need.

## 5. Personal devices
Personal phones must be silenced and stored during class unless the teacher allows their use for a learning activity.

## 6. Monitoring
Activity on district devices and the district network may be logged for safety and security. Logs are kept for 90 days.
