# 2026–27 Assessment Calendar and Exam Rules

## 1. Key dates
- Fall term mid-term exams: 19–23 October 2026
- Fall term final exams: 14–18 December 2026
- Spring term mid-term exams: 8–12 March 2027
- Spring term final exams: 1–7 June 2027

## 2. Exam rules
Phones, smartwatches, and earbuds must be stored in bags at the front of the room. Calculators are allowed only where the exam paper says so. AI tools are never allowed during exams.

## 3. Make-up exams
Students who miss an exam for illness or an approved absence may sit a make-up exam within ten school days. Requests go through the school counselor with a note from a parent, guardian, or doctor.

## 4. Accommodations
Students with an approved accommodation plan (for example, extra time or a separate room) receive it for every exam. Plans must be on file at least 15 school days before the exam window.

## 5. Results
Exam results are released through the student portal within ten school days after each exam window closes.
