# Student Data Privacy and AI

## 1. What counts as student data
Student data includes names, student ID numbers, dates of birth, home addresses, grades, attendance, health and disability information, disciplinary records, and any photos or recordings of students.

## 2. Rules for AI tools
Staff must never paste student data into an AI tool unless that tool is on the district's approved list **and** covered by a signed data processing agreement. Lakeview Assist and the enterprise chat assistant meet both conditions; consumer chatbots do not.

## 3. Data minimisation
When using an approved tool, staff should include only the data needed for the task. For example, to draft feedback on an essay, remove the student's name and ID first.

## 4. Retention
The district's AI vendors must delete prompts and outputs containing student data within 30 days and must not use them to train models. Conversation logs kept by the district for safety monitoring are retained for 90 days.

## 5. Special categories
Health information, disability status, and special-education records may never be entered into any AI tool, including approved tools.

## 6. Requests from families
Families may ask what AI tools their child used and what data was shared. Requests go to privacy@lakeview.example and must be answered within 30 days.

## 7. Incidents
If student data is entered into an unapproved tool, staff must report it to the Data Protection Office within 24 hours using the incident form. The office decides whether families must be notified.
