# Academic Integrity Policy

## 1. Expectations
Students are expected to submit work that reflects their own understanding, to credit sources, and to follow each assignment's rules about collaboration and tools.

## 2. Violations
Violations include copying another person's work, sharing answers during an assessment, using unauthorized materials, fabricating sources or data, and submitting AI-generated work as one's own.

## 3. Process
The teacher meets with the student to review the concern. The student may explain and present evidence such as drafts or version history. The teacher records the outcome in the student information system within five school days.

## 4. Outcomes
First violation: conference, parent or guardian notified, and an opportunity to redo the work for full credit at the teacher's discretion.
Second violation: the work receives a zero, and the student attends a restorative meeting with the assistant principal.
Third violation: referral to the principal; may affect eligibility for honors programs.

## 5. Appeals
Students or families may appeal an outcome in writing to the principal within ten school days. The principal responds within ten school days.
