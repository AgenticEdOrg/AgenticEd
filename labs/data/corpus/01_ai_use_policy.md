# Generative AI Use Policy

## 1. Purpose
This policy explains how students and staff in Lakeview Unified School District (LUSD) may use generative AI tools for learning and work. It applies to all schools, grades 6–12, and to district staff.

## 2. Approved tools
Students may only use AI tools on the district's approved list: the district-licensed AI assistant ("Lakeview Assist") and NotebookLM accessed through a district Google account. Staff may additionally use the district's enterprise chat assistant. Any other AI tool requires written approval from a teacher for a specific assignment.

## 3. Age requirements
Students under 13 may use approved AI tools only during supervised lessons on school devices. Students aged 13 and older may use approved tools at home for schoolwork.

## 4. Permitted uses
Students may use approved AI tools to brainstorm, get feedback on their own drafts, generate practice questions, and ask for explanations of difficult concepts. Teachers may use AI to draft lesson materials and rubrics, but must review every output before sharing it with students.

## 5. Prohibited uses
Students may not submit AI-generated text, code, or images as their own work; use AI during closed-book assessments; or create images, audio, or video of real people without their written consent. No one may use AI tools to make final decisions about grades, discipline, admissions, or special-education services.

## 6. Disclosure
Every graded assignment that used AI must include a short AI-use statement naming the tool and describing how it was used (for example, "Used Lakeview Assist to generate three practice questions on cell transport").

## 7. Accuracy
AI tools can produce confident but false information. Before relying on an AI-provided fact for graded work, students must verify it against a course textbook, a teacher, or another reliable source.

## 8. Consequences
Violations are handled under the Academic Integrity Policy. A first violation normally results in a conference with the teacher and an opportunity to redo the work. Repeated violations are referred to the school's assistant principal.

## 9. Review
The Office of Teaching and Learning reviews this policy every year in June, with input from the Student Advisory Council.
