## ✅ a4 (answerable): What is RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)?

**Answer:** Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) is a technique where relevant documents are fetched and provided to a language model so it answers using real information rather than relying only on its training data [1][2]. It acts like an open-book exam by retrieving passages related to a user's question and instructing the AI to answer directly from that material [1][2][3]. Grounding answers in these retrieved sources sharply reduces hallucinations, keeps information up to date, and enables citations [2][3].
