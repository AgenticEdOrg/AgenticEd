Privacy & Your Data

Your data stays yours

No accounts, no selling, no tracking across visits. Here's exactly what AgenticEd stores, where, and how to erase it.

The short version

  • AgenticEd needs no account to use.
  • Your worksheet answers and progress are saved only in your own browser (localStorage) — they never leave your device.
  • We don't sell or share your data with advertisers.
  • We count anonymous page visits (no name, no persistent ID) to show real activity on the homepage — see details below.
  • If you submit our feedback form, we store what you enter and use it only to improve AgenticEd or reply to you.
  • You can delete browser-saved learning data in one click below, and request deletion of submitted feedback by email.

What we store, and where

When you fill in a worksheet, complete an activity, or track your portfolio, that text is saved in your browser's localStorage so you don't lose it on refresh. It stays on your device and is visible only to you. Clearing your browser data — or using the button below — removes it.

Feedback submissions

When you choose to submit feedback, we store your visitor role, feedback category, course area, rating, message, submission time, and optional email address in our Supabase database. Providing an email is optional and is used only if you want a reply. Please do not submit student names, grades, or other sensitive information.

Feedback is reviewed by the AgenticEd team and used to improve the curriculum and website. It is not sold or shared with advertisers. To request deletion of a feedback submission, email hello@agenticed.org from the address included with the submission and describe the feedback.

Third-party AI tools

Some activities ask you to use free external tools (Claude, ChatGPT, NotebookLM, Perplexity). Those are separate services with their own privacy policies. Anything you type into them is governed by their terms, not ours — so treat them accordingly and follow the guidance below.

For teachers — FERPA & student data

Student education records are protected under FERPA. To stay on the safe side:

  • Have students use non-identifying example data — a made-up persona or a public document — never real names, grades, or records.
  • Confirm any AI tool is on your district's approved list before requiring student accounts.
  • Prefer the teacher-projected mode (one shared demo, no student logins) when in doubt.
