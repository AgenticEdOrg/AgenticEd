Six weekly missions.
Real evidence.

Each week combines a concept, experiment, activity, quiz, and portfolio artifact. Finish in six weeks or move at your own pace.

Week 1 · Open now

AI Foundations: From Automation to Agents

Activity: The Agent Hunt — map a real AI agent's full decision loop

Week 2

Generative AI & LLMs

Week 3

Tools, Retrieval & Memory

Week 4

Agent Workflows & Handoffs

Try it yourself

See how a language model picks its next word.

A model doesn't look answers up. It scores every possible next word and samples one. Move the temperature slider: low values make it predictable, high values make it adventurous — and more likely to drift.
